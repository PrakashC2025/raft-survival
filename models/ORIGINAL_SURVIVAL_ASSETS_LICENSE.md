# Original survival GLB assets

coastal_fish.glb, coastal_gull.glb, human_hand.glb, survival_bow.glb and coastal_ridge.glb were originally created for this project and are dedicated under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Source: tools/generate_survival_models.gd in this repository. Generated 2026-10-03 with Godot's GLTFDocument exporter. No downloaded geometry or textures were used. Static parts are merged offline; gull wing pivots remain separate for animation. This dedication applies only to these original assets, not supplied Sketchfab or other third-party content whose licenses appear in ASSET_CREDITS.md.
# Additional original wildlife and equipment (2026-10-05)

`wild_boar.glb`, `sea_turtle.glb` and `shore_crab.glb` are original modeled assets authored by `tools/generate_wildlife_models.gd`. `reef_shark.glb`, `raw_meat.glb`, `cooked_meat.glb` and `diving_tank.glb` are authored by `tools/generate_advanced_models.gd`. Machine geometry in `scripts/machine_visual.gd` is original project code; imported palm/grass licensing remains separate.

# Original survival GLB assets

coastal_fish.glb, coastal_gull.glb, human_hand.glb, survival_bow.glb and coastal_ridge.glb were originally created for this project and are dedicated under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Source: tools/generate_survival_models.gd in this repository. Generated 2026-10-03 with Godot's GLTFDocument exporter. No downloaded geometry or textures were used. Static parts are merged offline; gull wing pivots remain separate for animation. This dedication applies only to these original assets, not supplied Sketchfab or other third-party content whose licenses appear in ASSET_CREDITS.md.
# Additional original wildlife and equipment (2026-10-05)

`wild_boar.glb`, `sea_turtle.glb` and `shore_crab.glb` are original modeled assets authored by `tools/generate_wildlife_models.gd`. `reef_shark.glb`, `raw_meat.glb`, `cooked_meat.glb` and `diving_tank.glb` are authored by `tools/generate_advanced_models.gd`. Their original procedural textures and model-rendered `textures/ui/advanced_*.png` inventory images are included in this CC0 1.0 dedication. No downloaded model or reference-game art was copied. Machine geometry in `scripts/machine_visual.gd` is original project code; imported palm/grass licensing remains separate.

## Original runtime hunting and boat art (2026-10-15)

The crocodile, octopus and stone-tipped spear geometry authored in `scripts/hunting_visual.gd`, the open wooden hull/interior/seats/oars in `scripts/rowboat_visual.gd`, the ribbon weed/red algae/anemones in `scripts/underwater_reef_visual.gd`, and the original `shaders/rowboat_wood.gdshader` material artwork are also dedicated under CC0 1.0. They use original code-generated geometry and the project's existing credited material system. This dedication concerns that artwork and does not change the licenses of unrelated project code or third-party assets.

The active `hunting_crocodile.glb`, `hunting_boar.glb`, `hunting_rowboat.glb` and `hunting_workbench.glb` are downloaded/derived third-party artwork, **excluded from this CC0 dedication**. Their CC BY / CC BY-SA authorship, source URLs, changes and editable sources are listed in `ASSET_CREDITS.md`. The earlier original procedural crocodile/boar/hull remain fallback artwork only.

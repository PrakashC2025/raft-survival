# Photographic timber atlas

Generated for this project on 2026-10-07 with the built-in imagegen tool after the owner requested a realistic handcrafted wood feel. Original generated content; no downloaded timber photograph or reference-game art. The image is a photographic material sample, not a photograph of the game. Runtime geometry, wear, seams, normal relief, palette variation and lighting produce the final in-game appearance.

Final asset: `textures/construction/timber_photographic_atlas.png`. Actual image dimensions: 1254 by 1254 pixels (the generation prompt requested 2048). Four equal horizontal strips: salvaged pine, cedar, walnut and hemp. Imported with VRAM compression and mipmaps for desktop and Android. `wood_planks.gdshader` samples each strip with margins to avoid mip bleeding. `construction_visual.gd` builds shared rounded timber meshes while preserving the original sockets and collision sizes.

The earlier code-generated `timber_atlas.png` remains an optional fallback; `prepare_visual_overhaul.py` does not overwrite the selected photographic asset.

Full generation prompt (built-in tool, opaque background):

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: game-ready albedo texture atlas, 2048x2048 square, for a realistically handmade ocean survival raft. Primary request: a direct overhead macro photograph of real reclaimed weathered timber, NOT a render or illustration. Composition: exactly FOUR equal horizontal bands, each full-width and one quarter image height, no borders or labels. Top band: aged warm medium brown pine/driftwood, real wavy long horizontal grain with scattered irregular knots, small cracks and saw marks, abrasion and subtle salt bleaching; darker earthy brown rather than bright yellow/orange. Second band: weathered cedar, grey-brown with restrained warm red undertones, natural horizontal grain, fine fissures, occasional knots and subtle moisture stains. Third band: dark walnut reclaimed timber with realistic dark brown grain, rubbed edges and fine wear, not black. Fourth band: a neutral tan field of closely laid coarse hemp rope fibres oriented horizontally. Each band is a continuous material sample with NO board seams, no gaps, no nails, no hardware, no perspective. Grain predominantly runs LEFT TO RIGHT; natural organic variation and photographic microdetail, no even stripes, no cross-hatching, no toy/cartoon appearance, no glossy varnish. Flat neutral diffuse illumination suitable for a physically based albedo; no cast shadows, no specular highlights, no scenery, no objects, no text, no logos, no watermark. All four bands must touch with a straight clean horizontal boundary. Highly believable salvaged wood that looks hand crafted and exposed to seawater, warm muted earthy tones.
